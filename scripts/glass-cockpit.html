Design guide

Two glass surfaces, and they do different jobs. The panel is a clear pane that bends the game at its edges. The lens is a droplet that magnifies the selected channel. Everything else is flat paint on top.

Refraction
SVG feDisplacementMap as backdrop-filter. Its edge map is generated to the element's live size, so it never stretches.
Clarity
Blur only 2px and tint .24, so the game stays readable through the pane and the bending shows.
Thickness
A bright inset lip at the top-left, a softer one at the bottom-right, and a faint inner glow.
Selection
A glass droplet above the cards. It stretches 1.2× mid-slide and settles with a spring.
States
The lens tint follows the card: amber when selected, red when muted, blue when soloed. Solo adds the gold glint.
Fallback
If a browser can't do SVG backdrop filters, it gets frosted glass (blur 8px) automatically.
  • Two filtered surfaces, the panel and the small lens. The filter re-runs only over their area, and it's cheaper than the old 18–20px blur because the blur is now just 2px.
  • Edge maps are built once per size on a tiny canvas, not every frame.
  • All motion is transform and opacity: the open wobble, the lens slide and stretch, and the solo glint. Nothing changes layout while animating.
  • The app runs in WebView2 (Chromium), which supports SVG backdrop filters, so this is the look it will ship with.
